Watermark Studio

Stamp every design with your mark, then bind them into a project booklet. Runs in your browser, so your files stay on your computer.

Sample drawing shown. Add your own design images to replace it.
How to use
  1. Add your designsUse "Add images" or "Add a folder" under Design images, or drop files on the preview.
  2. Social media postClick Social post. Choose Instagram/Facebook, Square or TikTok, type the project name, then download this image or all social sizes.
  3. For clients or the websiteClick Client preview (stamp and QR) or Final delivery, then "Download all images (ZIP)".
  4. Project bookletFill in Booklet details, choose A4 landscape or portrait, then "Create project booklet (PDF)".

Always share TecArch designs with this watermark. Don't send clean, unwatermarked renders to anyone outside the office without approval.
